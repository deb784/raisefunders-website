Phase by Phase

What Happens at Each Stage

1
Weeks 1–2

Discovery & Asset Audit

Before we can package anything, we need to understand exactly what your organization has. This phase is about listening, learning, and documenting every asset that could have value to a local or regional sponsor.

What We Audit

Email list size & engagement, website traffic, social media channels and reach, events and tournaments, physical facilities, uniforms, and community loyalty signals.

What You Receive

A complete Asset Inventory Report documenting your monetizable assets, estimated market value, and a prioritization framework for packaging.

RaiseFunders Does

  • Conducts onboarding interview with key contact
  • Reviews all available digital and physical assets
  • Builds the Asset Inventory Report
  • Researches local sponsor market potential

Your Board Does

  • Participates in a 60–90 min onboarding call
  • Provides access to email platform & analytics
  • Reviews and approves the asset report
2
Weeks 3–4

Sponsorship Packaging

This is where your assets get transformed into sponsor-ready opportunities. We build tiered packages that make it easy for businesses to understand what they're buying, what it costs, and what they get in return.

What We Build

Tiered sponsorship packages (e.g., Presenting, Gold, Silver, Community Partner) with defined benefits, pricing, and professional presentation materials for each level.

What You Receive

A complete Sponsorship Package Deck and rate card, ready to present to potential sponsors. Clean, professional, and tailored to your organization's identity.

RaiseFunders Does

  • Designs all sponsorship tiers and pricing
  • Writes all package copy and benefit descriptions
  • Creates professional presentation materials
  • Develops the outreach target list

Your Board Does

  • Reviews and approves packages before outreach
  • Provides brand guidelines or logo files
  • Suggests any known local sponsor prospects
3
Weeks 4–12+

Outreach & Closing

This is where most organizations stall—and where RaiseFunders adds the most visible value. We manage the entire sponsor sales process from first contact to signed agreement, so your board never has to make a cold call.

Our Approach

Targeted outreach to local and regional businesses with a genuine connection to youth sports, families, and community. We use a structured follow-up cadence—not spam blasts.

How We Close

We handle negotiations, answer sponsor questions, prepare agreements, and guide prospects from interested to committed—keeping you informed at every milestone.

RaiseFunders Does

  • Executes full outreach and follow-up campaigns
  • Conducts all sponsor conversations and meetings
  • Negotiates terms and prepares agreements
  • Provides regular pipeline status updates

Your Board Does

  • Approves final sponsorship agreements
  • Signs contracts on behalf of the organization
  • Provides updates if circumstances change
4
Ongoing

Fulfillment Support & Reporting

Closing a sponsor is the beginning—not the end. Once a deal is signed, your organization executes the deliverables you agreed to—logos on jerseys, banners at the facility, sponsor recognition on your website, email footer credits, and event acknowledgments. We provide the tools, checklists, and reporting structure to make sure nothing falls through the cracks and that sponsors feel the value of their investment.

Fulfillment Tracking

We provide a structured checklist of every sponsor deliverable so your team knows exactly what needs to happen, when, and how to document it. Nothing gets lost between board rotations.

Sponsor Reporting & Renewals

Sponsors receive regular impact reports showing the value of their investment. We manage all renewal conversations and upsell opportunities—so sponsors come back year after year without any board effort.

RaiseFunders Does

  • Provides complete fulfillment checklist & tracking tools
  • Produces sponsor impact reports
  • Manages all renewal conversations and upsells
  • Supports your team with guidance throughout the season

Your Organization Does

  • Places sponsor logos on uniforms, banners & signage
  • Adds sponsor recognition to website & email footer
  • Executes event acknowledgments & social mentions
  • Reviews quarterly summary reports & approves renewals
