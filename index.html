The Reality

Most Youth Sports Organizations Have Sponsor Value They Never Fully Use

You're running a club, not a sales agency. But the sponsorship opportunity is sitting right there—untapped, unpackaged, and leaving money on the table every season.

Volunteer-Led Fundraising is Inconsistent

Fundraising depends on whoever has bandwidth this season. When that person moves on, the effort resets—and so does the revenue.

Sponsor Outreach Starts and Stops

Your board sends a few emails, gets a few responses, and the pipeline stalls. There's no repeatable process, no follow-up system, no closing.

Uncertainty About What You Can Actually Sell

Common Questions

Straight Answers to Your Biggest Questions

Is our club too small to qualify?
Size is less important than community reach and engagement. Many smaller clubs have highly loyal, tightly-knit communities that are extremely attractive to local sponsors. We assess each organization individually—a club with 80 engaged families can often outperform a club with 300 disconnected ones. Schedule a free discovery call and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.
What if we've never had sponsors before?
That's actually common—and completely workable. Starting from zero means there's no history to undo or legacy expectations to manage. We'll build your sponsorship program from the ground up with the right structure, the right pricing, and the right initial targets to get early wins that build momentum.
How much work does this create for our board?
Very little. That's the whole point. We handle the audit, the packaging, and the outreach right through closing. Your board's role is primarily to approve the sponsorship packages, provide access to your assets, and participate in a brief onboarding. After that, we run the program—you stay focused on your athletes.
How do you get paid?
Our model is a modest setup fee to cover onboarding and packaging work, combined with performance-based compensation tied to sponsorship revenue we help generate. This means our incentives are fully aligned with your success. We only do well when you do well—which is how it should be.
How long before we see results?
Most organizations see initial sponsor commitments within 60–90 days of completing the packaging phase. The timeline depends on your current asset base, the local sponsor market, and timing relative to your season. We'll give you a realistic projection during your discovery call—no inflated promises.
