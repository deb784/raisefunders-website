Our Clients

Built Specifically for Youth Sports Organizations

We don't serve everyone—we serve youth athletics. That focus makes us better at what we do and more credible to the sponsors you're trying to reach.

Who We Serve

Find Your Organization

RaiseFunders works with any youth sports organization that has community presence, engaged families, and assets worth packaging. Here's who we work with most.

Youth Athletic Clubs

Soccer, baseball, softball, basketball, hockey, lacrosse, volleyball, swimming—if you run a competitive youth club, you almost certainly have untapped sponsorship assets.

Strong Fit

Common Assets We Find

  • Large email lists of engaged sports families
  • Tournaments and showcase events
  • Team uniforms and practice gear
  • Social media channels with local reach
  • Facilities, fields, and gym signage

Associations & Leagues

Regional and local youth sports associations that govern multiple teams, manage registrations, and host league-wide events across a season.

Strong Fit

Common Assets We Find

  • Multi-team email databases across member clubs
  • Playoffs, championships, and all-star events
  • Association website and registration platform
  • Multiple facility locations across a region
  • High community name recognition

Booster Programs & Volunteer Boards

Parent-led booster clubs and volunteer boards that support a school or community athletic program and struggle with consistent fundraising year over year.

Strong Fit

Why Boosters Are a Great Fit

  • High volunteer burnout—this is exactly the problem we solve
  • Existing community trust and school-name recognition
  • Season events, banquets, and recognition nights
  • Student-athlete email/social following
  • Need for systems that survive board turnover

School-Adjacent Sports Groups

Club teams, travel programs, or feeder organizations connected to a school or district that operate independently but have strong school-community ties.

Strong Fit

What Makes These Organizations Attractive

  • Strong community identity and local brand recognition
  • Captive local family audience—sponsors love this
  • Active social media tied to school pride
  • Recurring events with high family attendance
  • Clearly defined geography for local sponsor targeting
Sound Familiar?

The Pain Points We Hear Most Often

We've had hundreds of conversations with club directors and booster leaders. These are the frustrations that come up in almost every call.

Volunteer Burnout is Real

"We keep asking the same five people to do everything. They're exhausted, and we lose them every couple of years."

Fundraising Resets Every Season

"Every year we're basically starting over. No process, no records, no relationships—just whoever steps up this season."

Skeptical of Upfront Costs

"We've been burned by consultants before. We paid for a plan and never saw results. We can't afford to gamble again."

Don't Know What to Sell

"We know we have some value to sponsors, but we don't know how to price it or present it in a way that gets a yes."

No One to Make the Calls

"We know we should be reaching out to businesses, but no one has the time—and cold calling isn't anyone's idea of fun."

Board Turnover Kills Progress

"Our best fundraiser left the board last spring. Everything she knew walked out with her. We're rebuilding from nothing."

If any of these hit close to home, we should talk.

Transparency Matters

We Might Not Be the Right Fit If…

We'd rather tell you upfront than waste your time. Here are situations where a RaiseFunders engagement may not make sense:

Your organization has fewer than 50 active families and limited community presence
You're looking for a one-time fundraising event, not a structured sponsorship program
Your board is unwilling to provide access to basic assets (email list, analytics)
You need immediate revenue this month—our process takes 60–90 days to see first results
Your organization is not a youth athletic program (we specialize exclusively in youth sports)
You're looking for a tool or software to manage sponsorships yourself—we are a people-powered service

Still not sure? Book a free call—we'll give you an honest answer about whether we're the right fit for your organization.
